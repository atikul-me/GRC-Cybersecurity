### 💡 Did You Know?

Before **2007**, the term **"GRC"** didn't even exist in the corporate or cybersecurity world\!

Back then, organizations handled **Governance**, **Risk Management**, and **Compliance** in completely separate silos—leading to duplicated efforts, blind spots, and massive inefficiencies.

In **2007**, the **Open Compliance and Ethics Group (OCEG)** officially coined and unified these three pillars into a single framework called **GRC** to help businesses achieve *"Principled Performance"*—especially critical in modern **Cybersecurity**, where security policies, cyber risks, and data laws must work together seamlessly.

---

### 🔍 Why It Matters in Cybersecurity?

Without a unified GRC, a company might buy expensive firewalls (Risk) without having a clear incident policy (Governance) or meeting data privacy laws like GDPR (Compliance).
